## 4. Fit-to-route zoom — numbers, so they are not re-derived

Recorded on **[#274](https://github.com/Valar-Systems/valar-scopes/issues/274)**.

```
R_max = 180 / (pi * DP_tolerance_degrees)
```

| density | `R_max` | visible cap |
|---|---|---|
| 0.50° (until today) | 115 | ±90° |
| **0.15° (current 4×)** | **382** | ±18.2° |

**The 4× regeneration bought 3.3× usable zoom and nobody priced it** — it was chosen for coastline smoothness at panel size. The zoom range came free and is worth more.

Clamp **R ∈ [120, 382]**. Below roughly **500 km** of route, a globe stops earning its place → **arc face**. #274's deleted threshold returns **derived, not picked**: it moves whenever the data does.

Gated behind this: state-line rendering and `ap:` airport markers (costing not yet done).
