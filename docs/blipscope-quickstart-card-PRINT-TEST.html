Printing this at home

  1. Paper: US Letter, Portrait. One card per sheet, front then back.
  2. Scale: 100% / "Actual size." Not "Fit to page" — that silently shrinks it.
  3. Margins: None (or Default; the card is centred either way).
  4. Headers and footers: off.
  5. Cut on the faint grey rectangle. Finished card is 4 × 6 in.
Tick “Background graphics” in the print dialog. Chrome leaves it off by default, and without it the numbered step badges print as white-on-white — steps 1, 2 and 3 vanish, and so do the green/amber/grey status dots on the back.
Check the ruler bar printed above each card. It must measure exactly 1.000 inch between the end ticks. If it doesn't, the print was scaled and the card is not 4 × 6 — fix the scale setting and print again.

Plug it in

Use a USB-C cable and any USB power adapter or computer port. The screen lights up in about three seconds.

2

Join its Wi-Fi

Point your phone's camera at the screen and tap Join. No prompt? Join the Wi-Fi network named on the screen. After you tap Join, the setup page takes a few seconds to appear.

A setup page opens. Choose your home Wi-Fi and enter your password. If no page opens, go to 192.168.4.1 in a browser.

3

Tell it where you are

Your Blipscope now shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone, or type the address under it into a browser on the same Wi-Fi.

On that page, tap Use my location and allow it. It saves on its own. Or copy your latitude and longitude from gps-coordinates.org into the Latitude box and click Save.

What the screen tells you

If updates stop arriving, Blipscope says so rather than freezing, and catches up on its own when data returns:

Sweeping — data is current
STALE 2m waiting on new data
NO DATA aircraft greyed out
If something’s wrong

Nothing on screen after saving

Location is probably still empty. Open the device’s address and check that Latitude and Longitude have numbers in them.

Changed your Wi-Fi, or moved house

Swipe left three times to Connect, tap [ Reset ], choose Reset Wi-Fi, then tap again to confirm.

Stuck on setup, or can’t reach it

Unplug it and plug it back in. The screen shows TOUCH & HOLD for three seconds — touch it and keep holding until it says WI-FI RESET. Then start again at step 2.

Screen works but taps do nothing

Unplug and replug. Still no touch? Email us your Device ID.
