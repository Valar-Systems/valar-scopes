Printing this at home

  1. Paper: US Letter, Portrait. One card per sheet, front then back.
  2. Scale: 100% / “Actual size.” Not “Fit to page.”
  3. Margins: None (or Default — the card is centred either way).
  4. Headers and footers: off.
  5. Cut on the faint grey rectangle. Finished card is 4 × 6 in.
Tick “Background graphics.” Chrome leaves it off by default — without it the numbered step badges print white-on-white and the gold claimed chips lose their fill, which is the one thing this card exists to show.
Check the ruler bar above each card. It must measure exactly 1.000 inch between the end ticks. If it doesn't, the print was scaled and the card is not 4 × 6.

Spotting leaderboard — optional

Pick a spotter name and your claim count ranks against every other opted-in scope; rarer types score higher. It sends your name, counts and type list — never your location. Off until you turn it on.

Alerts you can switch on
Watchlist
Callsigns, tails, ICAO hexes or whole type codes. Your phone buzzes when one goes over.
Look up!
A cyan ring when something crosses inside a distance you set.
Military
Orange pulsing ring, plus an optional phone alert.
Emergency
Red ring for a 7500 / 7600 / 7700 squawk. On by default.
Follow one
Draws its track; alerts on takeoff, landing and signal loss.
Make the screen yours

Sweep, fade, trails, altitude colour, airports and the MIL / HELI / SPC highlights toggle individually, in km or miles. Set the screen-top bearing so the top of the display matches your window — and it becomes a clock when the sky empties.
