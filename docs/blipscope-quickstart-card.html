BLIPSCOPE
Live aircraft radar for your desk

Setting up takes about three minutes.

1

Plug it in

Use the included cable and any USB power adapter or computer port. The screen lights up in about three seconds.

2

Join its Wi-Fi

The screen shows a hotspot name like Blipscope-326E20. Connect to it from your phone or laptop. There is no password.

A setup page opens. Choose your home Wi-Fi and enter your password.

3

Tell it where you are

The screen now shows an address like blipscope-326e20.local. Open it in a browser on the same Wi-Fi.

Go to gps-coordinates.org, enter your address, and copy the latitude and longitude. Paste them into the Latitude box — both boxes fill in. Click Save.

Aircraft appear within seconds.

Using it

Tap an aircraft for its registration, type, operator and route.

Swipe left for the aircraft list, again for stats and settings.

What the screen tells you

If updates stop arriving, Blipscope says so rather than showing a frozen picture:

Sweeping — data is current
STALE 2m waiting on new data
NO DATA aircraft greyed out

It catches up on its own the moment data returns.

If something’s wrong

Nothing on screen after saving

Location is probably still empty. Open the device’s address and check that Latitude and Longitude have numbers in them.

Changed your Wi-Fi, or moved house

Swipe left twice to Stats, tap [ Reset Wi-Fi ], then tap again to confirm.

Stuck on setup, or can’t reach it

Unplug it and plug it back in. The screen shows TOUCH & HOLD for three seconds — touch it and keep holding until it says WI-FI RESET. Then start again at step 2.
